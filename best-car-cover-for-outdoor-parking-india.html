What outdoor parking does to a car

A car parked outdoors faces rain, strong sun and constant dust. Sun fades paint and dries plastics, dust settles and can scratch when wiped, and rain can leave marks and let dirt dry on the surface. Bird droppings and tree sap add to the problem.

How to protect your car from dust and sunlight

Use a cover that is UV protected and dust proof, put it on a clean car, and make sure it is snug so dust cannot blow in at the sides. Park in shade when you can, and keep the cover clean.
