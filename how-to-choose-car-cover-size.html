How to choose the right car cover size

Choose a car cover by your exact make and model, not by a vague size label such as "medium" or "SUV". A cover made for your model fits snug from wheels to roof, with pockets for the mirrors, so it stays on in wind and does not rub the paint. Why size matters

A cover that is too small leaves the bumpers or wheels exposed and strains at the seams. One that is too big flaps in the wind, collects water in pools and can rub the paint. Both defeat the purpose of covering the car.

How to choose the size, step by step

  1. Note your exact make, model and body type (for example hatchback, sedan or SUV). Two cars from the same brand can differ a lot in length and roof height.
  2. Choose a cover made for that model rather than a generic size. Model-specific covers account for mirrors, antenna and body shape.
  3. Check the fit points: the cover should reach the ground-level skirt over the wheels, sit close at the mirrors, and cover both bumpers.
  4. If your car has accessories such as a roof rack, a spoiler or a shark-fin antenna, tell the seller before ordering.
  5. Confirm before you pay. Send the model name and a photo if it is unusual, and ask for the fit to be confirmed in writing.
