Why Bangalore cars need a proper cover

Bangalore cars sit outside more than most people admit: apartment parking, street parking, office lots. That means monsoon rain, strong sun and constant dust on the paint. A thin generic sheet does little against any of them, and a loose one can flap in the wind and rub the paint.
